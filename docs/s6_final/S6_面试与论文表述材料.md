# S6 面试与论文表述材料

## 1. One-minute Project Explanation

我在 MiniOneRec 的生成式推荐系统里做了一个 validation-only 的 S6 研究：用完整 SASRec 模型直接做用户条件化 item retrieval，替代原来昂贵的第二个 SASRec-SID Qwen 生成流。结果显示，Direct SASRec 在 4532 行 validation 上提供了 236 个 CF candidate pool 之外的 target，K20 被选为最小通过候选预算；它的候选 pipeline wall time 只有 SASRec-SID Qwen beam-50 的约 5.17%，对应 19.36x wall-clock factor。排序层面，S6-5 frozen projected ranker 把 HR@20 提升到 0.141659，但 valid_select CF preservation 只有 95.79%，低于 97% gate。因此最终结论是 retrieval GO、ranking partial closeout，而不是 production-ready。

## 2. Three-minute S6 Explanation

S5 使用 CF-SID Qwen 主流和 SASRec-SID Qwen 辅助流。SASRec-SID 的问题是，它把 SASRec item embedding 聚类成 SID，然后还要训练或调用第二个 Qwen 去生成这些 SID。这条路径既贵，也不等于完整 SASRec 推荐器，因为只用了静态 item embedding。

S6 的核心假设是：如果恢复完整 SASRec forward，先由用户历史得到序列表示 `z_h`，再对所有 item embedding `e_i` 计算 `score(i | h) = z_h^T e_i`，就能得到更直接、更便宜的行为候选。我们验证了 checkpoint 完整性、候选输出确定性、K20/K50/K100 prefix consistency、CF + Direct union provenance，以及 frozen ranker 的 ranking conversion。

结果分两层。候选层成功：K20 union target-in-pool 从 CF 的 629 提升到 865，新增 236 个 target。成本层成功：Direct pipeline wall 34.23 秒，Qwen beam-50 pipeline wall 662.68 秒，Direct 约为 Qwen 的 5.17%。排序层部分成功：S6-5 projected ranker aggregate HR@20 为 0.141659，NDCG@20 为 0.088344，但 valid_select CF preservation 只有 91/95 = 95.79%，没过 97% gate。后续 S6-6 和 S6-6R 两个轻量修正也没过 gate，所以停止继续调参。

## 3. Full Technical Narrative

S6 把“候选召回是否值得保留”和“排序器是否已经 production-ready”分开。Direct SASRec 的候选召回验证是正结果；ranking gate 是 partial。这个拆分很重要，因为 candidate oracle 只说明 target 在 pool 中，不说明最终 ranker 能把 target 排进 top20。

S6 的技术路径包括：

1. 恢复 formal-v3 SASRec checkpoint，验证 item embedding、position embedding、attention、FFN、norm 和 cold-item fallback。
2. 用完整 forward 得到用户历史表示。
3. 对全量 item 做 dot product scoring，稳定 top-K。
4. 一次 K100 推理派生 K20/K50/K100 prefix views。
5. 与 frozen CF split views deterministic union，保留 provenance。
6. 用 S5 frozen projected ranker 做 source-independent projection。
7. 尝试 S6-6 residual ranker 和 S6-6R CF-anchored promotion gate。
8. 用 v2 Qwen cost profile 做 validation-side wall-clock 对比。

最强可防守结论：Direct SASRec 是一个经过正式验证、低成本、互补性强的候选源；但当前 S6 没有得到满足严格 CF preservation gate 的新 ranking policy。

## 6. Frequently Asked Interview Questions

### Why replace SASRec-SID Qwen?

Because SASRec-SID Qwen requires a second generative decoding pipeline. It is expensive and only asks Qwen to generate clustered item identifiers derived from SASRec embeddings. Direct SASRec uses the recommender's own user-conditioned scoring function.

### Why not use only SASRec embeddings?

Item embeddings alone are static. Recommendation requires a user-history representation. SASRec computes `z_h` from the sequence, then scores items with `z_h^T e_i`.

### Why must the full SASRec forward be restored?

Without position embeddings, attention blocks, FFN and normalization, there is no sequence-conditioned representation. The model would collapse into static item similarity rather than sequential recommendation.

### How does SASRec produce user-conditioned item scores?

Given history `h`, SASRec produces final hidden state `z_h`. Each item has embedding `e_i`. The score is:

```text
score(i | h) = z_h^T e_i
```

Then all legal items are sorted by score.

### How is this different from Text-SID and CF-SID?

Text-SID and CF-SID encode item identity into symbolic IDs for generative decoding. Direct SASRec does not generate a SID; it scores item IDs directly using behavior-sequence representation.

### Why select K20?

K20 was the smallest K passing candidate uplift gates on valid_fit and valid_select. K50/K100 had larger pool recall but were retained only as ablations to avoid unnecessary ranking burden.

### Why not use K100 when its pool recall is larger?

Because more candidates increase candidate noise and ranking difficulty. The protocol selected the smallest K that passed the gate.

### Why does candidate recall not directly equal HR@20?

Candidate recall only says the target exists in the pool. HR@20 requires the ranking policy to place it in the top20.

### Why does Direct order have high NDCG but union ranking remains difficult?

Direct order is strong for behavior-like targets but can displace CF hits. Union ranking must preserve CF top20 wins while recovering direct-only targets.

### Why does fixed RRF underperform the projected ranker?

Fixed RRF uses a simple rank combination rule. The projected ranker uses learned source-independent history and candidate features, so it converts more auxiliary recall.

### Why was the frozen ranker allowed to use projected auxiliary features?

It was a frozen S5 ranker evaluated under source-independent projection. Direct raw scores were excluded to avoid changing its semantic contract.

### Why were direct raw-score features excluded from the frozen ranker?

Because the frozen ranker was not trained on direct score semantics. Including raw direct scores would silently change the feature distribution and invalidate the frozen contract.

### Why did the residual ranker fail?

It could exploit direct score features, but the CF head quota created a sharp trade-off: aggressive settings lost CF hits; conservative settings lost direct recovery.

### Why did the promotion gate fail?

It protected CF ordering better, but direct promotion remained too blunt. High promotion recovered direct targets but broke CF preservation; conservative promotion underused auxiliary recall.

### Why stop tuning?

The protocol declared S6-6R as the final lightweight revision. Continuing after repeated fit/select failures would become validation-set overfitting.

### Was valid_gate used for tuning?

No. S6-6 and S6-6R did not pass fit/select, so their valid_gate remained unopened.

### Was test used?

No. S6 is validation-only; test remained unopened.

### Is 19.36x a fair comparison?

It is fair as a candidate-pipeline wall-clock comparison on the same 4532 validation rows and A100-class evidence. It is not a pure model-kernel speedup.

### Can this be called production-ready?

No. Candidate retrieval is validated, but ranking is partial because strict CF preservation was not satisfied.

### What is the strongest defensible conclusion?

Direct SASRec is a validated, much cheaper, complementary auxiliary candidate generator. The ranking layer needs new research before production replacement.

### What would be the next research improvement?

A ranker trained explicitly for CF preservation and direct recovery, such as listwise ranking with preservation constraints or multi-task loss.

## 7. Aggressive Interviewer Questions

### Are you just moving cost from Qwen to SASRec?

No. Direct SASRec uses one sequential model forward and matrix scoring, while SASRec-SID Qwen uses beam-50 autoregressive generation plus constrained decoding and candidate evaluation. The measured pipeline wall time is 34.23 s versus 662.68 s.

### Did you cherry-pick K20?

No. K20 was selected by a predeclared smallest-K-passing-gate rule. K50/K100 are documented as ablations.

### Did you tune on test?

No. S6 used only validation splits. The final verdict explicitly prohibits test-set tuning.

### Why not declare success if S6-6 aggressive has better HR?

Because it fails CF preservation on valid_select. The goal is fixed CF strength plus auxiliary recovery, not HR alone.

## 8. Limitations and Honest Answers

- S6 does not prove production readiness.
- S6 does not provide a gate-complete ranker.
- S6 cost result is pipeline wall-clock, not pure kernel timing.
- S6 validation population is limited to 4532 validation rows.
- S6 does not integrate direct retrieval into online serving.
